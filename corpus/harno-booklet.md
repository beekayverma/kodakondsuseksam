The examination on the knowledge of the
Constitution of the Republic of Estonia and
the Citizenship Act (Examination) is designed for
those who wish to apply for Estonian citizenship.

The examination is held 12 times a year in
Tallinn, Narva, Jõhvi and Tartu and twice a
year in Pärnu, Rakvere and Valga.
The examination takes place on the same
date and at the same time at all the examination centres.
Taking the examination is free of charge.

examination. The consultations are open to all
who wish to learn about the content and format
of the examination and check their preparation
for the examination. A notice of the time and
place of the consultations and the examination is
sent to the registered participants 7 days before
the examination at the latest.
It is a written examination with 24 multiple
choice questions. For each question, the one correct answer must be marked. All the questions
are in Estonian. You have 45 minutes to take the
examination.

The examination questions involve
the following areas:
- The fundamental principles of the Estonian
form of government, stipulated in Chapters
I and III of the Constitution of the Republic
of Estonia;
- The fundamental rights, freedoms and duties of each person, stipulated in Chapter
II of the Constitution of the Republic of
Estonia;
- The jurisdiction of the Riigikogu, the
President of the Republic, the Government of the Republic and the courts
according to the Constitution of the
Republic of Estonia;
- The conditions and procedure for
the acquisition, resumption and
loss of Estonian citizenship according to the Citizenship Act.
